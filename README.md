It's time to get that degree from the Fairy Academy. But to become a ***SIMPLIFAIRY*** is to control the battlefield with the bare necessities: your wand. No powers will help you other than your pure whimsical fairy skills.

After each wave, you will have to choose an ability to sacrifice, which continuously narrows your toolkit and simplifies your playstyle in subsequent rounds until it is "as simple as possible".

[**Simplifairy**](https://stratisra.itch.io/simplifairy) was designed in 72 hours and submitted to [Mini Jam 217: Whimsy](https://itch.io/jam/mini-jam-217-whimsy) where it received **13th** place out of 95 entries.

The **limitation** of the game jam was: **As simple as possible**
